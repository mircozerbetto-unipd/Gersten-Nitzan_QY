# Gersten-Nitzan_QY
Jupyter notebook to calculate the quantum yield enhanchement due to coupling of fluorphore to the plasmon of a metal nanoparticle in proximity of the dye. The calculation is based on Gersten-Nitzan modeling in case of spherical nanoparticles.

For usage, just open the notebook in Jupyter or in Google Colab. The notebbok is self explicative and provides references.
